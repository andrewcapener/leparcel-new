# Mark Cross — Initial Ads Audit · 2026-09-02

Pulled live from the Google Ads API via `https://housework-api.vercel.app/api/markcross-audit`
(read-only route, deployed today). Meta could not be audited — see §4.

## 1. Headline findings

1. **Google conversion tracking is broken, and it inflates everything.** The account
   counts **"Google Shopping App Begin Checkout"** as a primary conversion alongside
   **"Google Shopping App Purchase"** (both `primary: true`, both counting in the
   conversions metric). Result: the brand Shopping campaign reports **414 conversions
   worth $1.17M on $5,880 spend in 30 days ("198x ROAS")** — not credible even at
   Mark Cross price points. Every bidding decision (both live Shopping campaigns run
   Target ROAS) is optimizing against these corrupted numbers. Meanwhile the real GA4
   purchase action (`MarkCross.com - GA4 (web) purchase`) exists but is **not primary
   and not counted**. **Fix this before touching budgets — nothing else in the account
   can be evaluated until conversions mean purchases.**

2. **Only 3 of 125 campaigns are actually spending** (~$10.3k over the last 30 days).
   The other 122 are paused/removed debris from at least four previous
   agencies (naming eras: `290219 |`, `GS_`, `MarkCross_*_SEM/PerformanceMax`,
   `Mark Cross - *`). Three more are ENABLED but serving nothing
   (`GS_BR_Products BR Category eCPC`, `MarkCross_Brand_DemandGen`,
   `Mark Cross - Branding - Search - US Manual CPC`) — likely no eligible ads or
   ended schedules; they should be paused or fixed deliberately.

3. **Both live Shopping campaigns sit at ~10% search impression share.** Whatever the
   true ROAS is, there is significant headroom — but scaling on a Target ROAS fed by
   fake conversion values would scale blindly.

4. **Cross-client contamination:** a campaign named **"Modish Custom Homes - Google
   Build"** (paused) lives inside the Mark Cross account. A previous operator built an
   unrelated business's campaign here. Cosmetic, but worth removing.

  whether that clears breakeven depends on margin (need the client's number).
